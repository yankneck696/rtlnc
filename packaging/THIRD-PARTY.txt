rtlnc bundles the following third-party components:

* RTL-SDR Blog driver library (rtlsdr.dll), GPL-2.0-or-later
  Source: https://github.com/rtlsdrblog/rtl-sdr-blog
* libusb-1.0, LGPL-2.1-or-later. Source: https://github.com/libusb/libusb
* Python runtime (PSF License), NumPy and SciPy (BSD), pyrtlsdr (GPL-3.0),
  pyserial (BSD), python-sounddevice (MIT) with PortAudio (MIT).

rtlnc itself is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later (see LICENSE), which is compatible
with the bundled components. Full license texts are available at the source
links above.
